Cookie & Storage Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Marzoft Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") operates as a high-performance digital engineering studio. We prioritize user privacy and system transparency. This policy explains how our technology uses browser storage and external scripts.

1. OUR PRIVACY COMMITMENT

We do not track, collect, or share your personal data for advertising, marketing, or third-party retargeting. We never monetize user information. We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral cookies, or ad-targeting beacons.

2. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE?

  • ▹ Cookies are small data files saved by web browsers to maintain functional states.
  • ▹ LocalStorage / SessionStorage are high-performance memory buckets inside your browser that allow our apps to run smoothly.

3. WHY WE USE STORAGE AND EXTERNAL SCRIPTS

We use lightweight external scripts and browser storage exclusively for technical performance and functional stability:

  • ▹ Immersive UI Performance: We load external script libraries via secure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)—specifically Three.js (for 3D rendering), GSAP (for smooth animation pipelines), and Lenis (for inertial scrolling). These tools require minimal browser resources to deliver cinematic, hardware-accelerated visuals.
  • ▹ Data Persistence & State: We use LocalStorage to keep your personalized changes alive when you navigate between our demo pages or refresh the browser. This ensures your customized inputs (like calculator state or demo parameter selections) are not wiped out.
  • ▹ Secure Cloud Handshakes: We utilize Supabase as our secure database backend. When you interact with our SaaS dashboards, browser storage holds temporary, encrypted authentication tokens to keep your session secure while you manage your workspace.

4. YOUR CONTROL

You have full authority over your device's security. You can wipe your browser cache, block LocalStorage, or disable functional tokens anytime within your browser’s advanced settings.
