Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Marzoft Technologies builds custom web designing frameworks and advanced SaaS web products. Because we commit immediate development resources, purchase digital properties, and write customized system logic from day one, we adhere to a clear balance standard based on explicit cancellation parameters.

1. CLIENT-INITIATED CANCELLATION RULES

Clients maintain the right to issue a project cancellation request or terminate their recurring SaaS product profiles at any time. Intents must be formally delivered to our development team in writing.

  • ▹ Custom Web Designing Deposits: The 50% advance deposit collected at project kickoff is STRICTLY 100% NON-REFUNDABLE under client-initiated terms. Upon receiving your advance via UPI, funds are immediately deployed to permanently buy out your custom domain name extension (.com, .in, .org, or .io) on global registries and clear schedule space.
  • ▹ Radical Scope Shifts: If a client completely updates or changes their entire core business concept midway through development (e.g., transforming a Gym landing page project into an active Cloud Kitchen SaaS tool), the initial advance is permanently forfeited. Because development time was uniquely spent on the first framework structure, a new concept path requires a separate contract and a new 50% advance deposit.

2. SaaS PRODUCT MONTHLY CANCELLATION PROTOCOL

SaaS platform usage cycles operate purely via manual, user-driven peer-to-peer UPI or QR transfers. Because there are no automated recurring card subscriptions, any monthly renewal balance sent to our UPI endpoints is considered entirely deliberate. Consequently, completed subscription payments are immediately final with zero refund options, and accounts remain active for use until the prepaid cycle block finishes.

3. MARZOFT-INITIATED CANCELLATION RULES (WORK-DONE PRO-RATA PROTECTION)

In the rare event that Marzoft Technologies must cancel a project from our side (such as unforeseen emergency timeline blocks or handling toxic, counter-productive communications), a strict pro-rata rule applies to protect initial costs:

  • ▹ Core Work Evaluation: We will assess the total volume of frontend layout logic and staging previews already engineered at the time of the notice.
  • ▹ Financial Adjustment: Marzoft will retain a flat 25% work-done fee from the total contract amount to absorb the unrecoverable domain registry costs and initial labor hours spent formatting the design components. The remaining 25% balance from the client's upfront deposit will be completely returned via direct UPI transfer.

4. DOMAIN RECOVERY ASSURANCE

In the event of a client-initiated cancellation, while the initial deposit is forfeited, any custom standalone domain name registered on your behalf during the kickoff phase remains entirely under your personal brand ownership settings for its designated 1-year registry lifecycle.
